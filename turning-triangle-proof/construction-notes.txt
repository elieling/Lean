THE TWO-RIGHT-TRIANGLE TURNING PROOF

Main source: TurningTriangleProof.lean
Main theorem: Turning.lem_turning_by_triangles
Transport to any supplied isometric frame:
  Turning.lem_turning_by_triangles_isometric_copy

This follows the manuscript's two-triangle contradiction, rather than the
alternative 7/15 argument in LocalGrid.lean. There is no hgeometry hypothesis.

Construction and the necessary index correction

The supplied figure specifies inward perpendiculars: a=v, b lies on outgoing m,
and e lies on the extension of incoming l. The angle between the two forward
rays is beta=pi-alpha. The full perpendicular segments have length 2n^2 epsilon.
The source fixes n>=2 and epsilon=1/(4n^8).

For each x in {1,...,n^2+1}, put s=x epsilon and choose auxiliary c,d at inward
distance s from the two attachment points. This is possible because
  s <= (n^2+1)epsilon <= 2n^2 epsilon.
Then all four triangle legs have length s. The manuscript's equality of all
four lengths with (n^2+1)epsilon is true only at the largest index and is
replaced by this corrected statement. The bound s<=1/2 remains valid.

Proof correspondence

1. EqualLegTriangle.lean proves the two legs really have hyperbolic length s,
   the foot angle is pi/2, and each diagonal geodesic reaches its endpoint at
   positive time. Actual unit tangent vectors determine the vertex angles:
     beta_1=beta_2=arctan(tanh(s)/sinh(s))=arctan(1/cosh(s)).
   Reflection supplies the second triangle.

2. TurningSeries.lean proves the exact Taylor-series comparison in the text:
     cosh(s) = sum s^(2k)/(2k)!
             <= sum (s^2/2)^k = 1/(1-s^2/2) <= 8/7.
   The geometric-series convergence condition is explicit and proved.

3. The original arctangent identity is retained:
     arctan(7/8)=pi/4-arctan(1/15)>pi/4-1/15>2pi/9.
   Hence beta_1 and beta_2 both exceed 2pi/9.

4. TriangleSeparation.lean proves that disjoint inward segments force
     beta_1+beta_2<beta.
   This is derived from the explicit geodesics, tangent monotonicity and an
   intermediate-value argument. It does not invoke the numerical angle bound,
   the earlier 7/15 argument, or an already proved turning-intersection theorem.

5. TurningTriangleProof.lean finishes the contradiction:
     beta > beta_1+beta_2 > 4pi/9 > 4pi/15 >= beta.

Formal model and scope

The sets lGrid and mGrid are points of mathlib's UpperHalfPlane with its actual
Poincare metric. HyperbolicMetric.lean proves exact distances for the radial
and perpendicular parameterizations. Angles use the hyperboloid unit-tangent
formula arccos(-lorentz(v,w)), with actual derivatives and diagonal geodesics
certified by HyperbolicAngles.lean and EqualLegTriangle.lean.

The normalized construction and all its images under a supplied hyperbolic
isometry are covered. A separate theorem normalizing arbitrary pre-existing
finite segments l,m into this frame is not included. Attachment points must
exist on their supporting geodesics, as stipulated in the construction.
The rest of the graph reduction, gadget grids and propagation lemmas are not
part of this turning lemma.

Earlier files remain as dependencies and historical alternatives. In particular,
the conditional wrappers in Turning.lean are not used by the new theorem.
